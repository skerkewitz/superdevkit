## Purpose

The milestones, their state, and the open questions, in a form a user can read without the
working history of `docs/roadmap.md`.

## Sections

1. **Milestones** – a table: milestone, goal, state. One paragraph each for the one in progress
   and the next.
2. **The checkpoint after M2** – the decision that the compiler and the game work in parallel,
   with the numbers that led to it.
3. **Risks and open questions** – from the roadmap, those a user would care about.

## Pay attention to

- Leave out the step-by-step history; keep what is decided and what is open.
- Plans are allowed here, unlike on the other pages.

## Out of scope
